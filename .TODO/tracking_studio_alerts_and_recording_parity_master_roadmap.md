> **Architecture Reference**: `docs/architecture/IDEAL_WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md` (`ADRs 01–23`, Layers 1–5) & `docs/architecture/CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` (branch `feature/debug-session-applications`, `HEAD` `251471c`, `OBS-023`–`OBS-048` + `DBG-01`–`DBG-04`).
> **How to use this document**: Execute one Step at a time via child plans linked in `.frontier/` (`--parent tracking_studio_alerts_and_recording_parity_master_roadmap --milestone <N>`). Only Step 1 is detailed into a child plan upfront; Steps 2–4 are deferred and planned one step at a time. Every Step enforces modularity budgets (`<= 350L` soft / `< 500L` hard, `<= 12` methods/class, `<= 40L`/fn, routes `<= 25L`) and a mandatory **Review Gate** (a fresh reviewer separate from the author inspects the diff and verify output before advancing).
> **Decision Basis (Decision 1 locked + Axiom 10 defaults for Steps 2–4)**:
> 1. **Alerts Noise & Promotion Rules (Decision 1, `ADR-23`)**: Keep `world_entity_state_changed` in-memory on `EventBus` for live `WorldGraph` reactivity (do not persist periodic `0.5s` ticks into SQLite `world_timeline_events`), wire `EventBus.set_write_through_handler`, persist explicit `severity` (`critical`/`warning`/`info`) + canonical unscoped `zone_id` + `zone_dwell`/`zone_exit` start/end/duration, and add DB-backed Alert Promotion Rules (`WorldPolicy` / `AlertRuleEngine`) in `#alerts` (`OBS-030`–`034`, `DBG-04`).
> 2. **Live Recording & Alert Clip Provenance (`ADR-23`, assumed Recommended)**: Prefer web-playable H.264 (`avc1`) with automatic async `_h264.mp4` transcode fallback on recording `stop()`, guard `POST /api/videos/{filename}/transcode` against actively recording files (`409` before FFmpeg sees missing `moov` atom), offload `ffprobe` via `asyncio.to_thread`, and stamp `(video_filename, media_offset_sec)` on live alerts/sightings during recording (`OBS-038`–`041`, `DBG-03`).
> 3. **Tracking Stability, Clean Studio & Calibrated World Map (`ADRs 22–23`, assumed Recommended)**: Fix `record_zone_exit(track_id=...)` `TypeError`, align `CANONICAL_REID_DIM` dynamically (`2560D`), add centroid fallback + hide stale coasting ghosts in `SortTracker`/telemetry, enforce per-frame 1-to-1 entity uniqueness, attach `#studio-live-img` with `?annotated=false` + live redraw loop, open full Zone/Tripwire edit modals, and stagger uncalibrated cameras + derive `CameraWorldPose`/`fov_corners_global` on homography save (`DBG-01`, `DBG-02`, `OBS-024`–`029`, `OBS-035`, `OBS-036`, `OBS-040`, `OBS-047`).
> 4. **Review Triage, Filtered Heatmaps & HUD Split (`ADR-23`)**: Add `total_pending`, pagination, filters, toast `review_id` deep-links, and bulk-dismiss to `#review` (`OBS-043`, `OBS-048`), add filtered historical DB heatmap mode to `#analytics` (`OBS-023`), and track `system-diagnostics` Debug HUD enhancements (`OBS-037`, `OBS-042`, `OBS-044`–`046`) in standalone `.TODO/debug_hud_picker_filter_sort_and_drag_plan.md`.
